An interactive project within the ArchiWatts portfolio

Hive · drawings, space, and material

A capstone proposal at 2301 NW 33rd Avenue, Miami. The base addresses the neighborhood and canal; the elevated amenity deck connects the residences and becomes a future ground in the sea-level-rise studies.

Plans: Grade and L-10 from the supplied plans PDF, plus the separate amenity drawing, are registered to matching model slab edges. Residential levels reuse the typical L-10 footprint. Office and roof outlines are traced from the model; they are not separate supplied floor-plan sheets.

Views: Paired render studies offer approximate camera positions alongside the original finished images. Textured mode uses concrete and paving microtextures, cool facade panels, dark perforated screens, reflective glass, and environment lighting, interpreted from those renders. Diagram mode is optional.

Source references: Hive.pdf pp.12–15 and 28–39; Portfolio 2023 pp.5–8; Grade/L-10 plan sheets; AMENITYS drawing; supplied PNG renders. Phase 1 is the neighborhood edge, Phase 2 the waterfront, Phase 3 the connector. Phase model selections follow spatial block assignments and may include shared elements. Original Revit materials were not exported; this is a real-time material interpretation, not the original render engine. Plan alignment follows slab edges, not survey registration. Walk remains free navigation.
