01

The neighborhood and the waterfront

Hive organizes its proposal around three spatial phases: the neighborhood edge, the waterfront, and the connector. The model lets these parts be read together or isolated to understand their relationships.

02

An elevated shared landscape

Above the office areas, the amenity deck links the residential buildings. Pavilions punctuate this rooftop landscape. In the sea-level-rise studies, the elevated deck is explored as a future ground.

03

Drawing and inhabiting

Floor plans and paired render views connect the building’s organization to its spatial experience. Open the model to compare levels, inspect the amenity pavilions, and move between the overall composition and an eye-level view.
