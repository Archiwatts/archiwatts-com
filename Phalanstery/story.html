Scope
Perimeter enclosure, city cluster, elliptical building, and water routes
01

A collective composition

The continuous perimeter establishes a shared enclosure. Within it, a dense cluster of towers contrasts with the elliptical volume and the isolated tower across the water.

02

Color as orientation

Cream surfaces, blue volumes, red towers, and turquoise water distinguish the parts of the composition. Repeated window openings establish a rhythm at the scale of the facade.

03

From axonometric to street

The original architectural views move between the whole arrangement and framed encounters within it. The interactive study extends that sequence through orbit views and walking, with moving figures and gondolas providing a sense of scale.
