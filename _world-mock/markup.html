Mark this up so I can see what you mean

Two plans of the mock as it stands, then small sketches of different ways to blend the city. Nothing here is final. Pin anything, anywhere.

How to mark up: press Annotate (bottom left), click the exact spot on a plan or on a sketch, type your note and save. Each pin remembers where you clicked, so "move this here" or "this edge is wrong" works. Rough notes are fine.

Questions I still have

  1. Seams. When Hive's neighbourhood meets the school's fields, should you notice the change (two places stitched together) or should it feel like one place that was always there?
  2. Real or invented. Is it acceptable for me to invent new ground, streets and filler buildings between your sites, or should every piece of the city come from one of your real site models?
  3. Orientation. May I rotate a site to make it fit (for example turn the Youth Center so its real beach faces open water), or must every site keep true north?
  4. Water. Hive's canal and the two stretches of the Thames run through the city. Should they join into one continuous waterway you could sail through?
  5. The two London farms. They are two versions of one project on the same real site. Should each keep its own copy of the river, or should they stand on opposite banks of one shared river?
  6. Venice. Should it stay a separate island reached by a bridge, or sit so close to the city that it reads as the old quarter of it?
  7. Arrival. When someone first opens the world, where do they start: high above everything, on the promenade, or arriving by boat?
  8. Walking. True scale makes the city about 1.5 km across, roughly 20 minutes on foot. Do you want real walking speed with a fast-travel option, or a quicker glide everywhere?
  9. Look. Should the surrounding city stay grey with only your projects in colour, or should the whole city carry colour and material?
