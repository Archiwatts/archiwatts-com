      I am a designer in Miami working across architecture, graphic design, and experimental art. This site collects my architectural studies, interactive models, and project stories.

      Philosophy

      A strong designer-client relationship hinges on empathy and understanding. As designers, it’s vital to step into the shoes of our clients and end-users. This empathetic approach allows us to comprehend their needs, desires, and challenges fully. Just as sustainable design is crucial for the environment, universal design is equally important as it ensures that our creations are accessible, functional, and inclusive for all. By putting ourselves in the client’s position, we not only deliver solutions that truly resonate with them but also foster trust, effective communication, and ultimately, a successful collaboration that stands the test of time.
