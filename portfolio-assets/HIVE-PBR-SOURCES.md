1K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. Color maps adapted to the selected light oak, white concrete and tinted plaster finishes. WebP versions shared across meshes; no displacement geometry added. Color is sRGB; normals/roughness use linear data. Full original downloads retained under work/hive-free-pbr.
